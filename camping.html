❓ FAQs

Is it safe for families?
Yes! The camp is fully fenced, guarded 24/7, and has CCTV. We have separate family sections.
Are there washrooms?
Luxury Swiss Tents & Cottages have attached western washrooms. Alpine tents use clean common washrooms.
Is alcohol allowed?
We do not sell alcohol. Guests can bring their own for private consumption inside tents.
