Know Your Rapids

Which grade is right for you?

Grade I & II (Beginner)

Small waves, clear passages. Safe for non-swimmers, kids (14+), and seniors. Relaxing and fun. (Found in 9KM & 12KM trips).

Grade III (Thrill)

Bigger waves that crash into the boat. The boat might rock! Requires paddling. The most popular choice. (Found in 16KM & 26KM trips).

Grade IV (Extreme)

Massive waves, difficult passages, and strong currents. Chance the boat could flip. Requires fitness. (Found in 36KM trip).

Rafting FAQs

Can non-swimmers do rafting?
Yes! You get a high-buoyancy Life Jacket that keeps you afloat. Non-swimmers can safely do Grade I, II, and III.
Minimum Age?
14 years for 9KM. For 16KM and above, we recommend 16+ years.
Is it open in Monsoon?
No, rafting is closed from July to mid-September due to high water levels.
