Rental FAQs

Is fuel included?
No, the vehicle is provided with enough fuel to reach the nearest petrol pump. You must fill it yourself based on your usage.
Is there a deposit?
Yes, a refundable security deposit (usually ₹1000-₹2000 per vehicle) is required. It is returned immediately when you hand back the bike safely.
What is the timing?
A "Day" rental usually counts from morning (9 AM) to evening (8 PM). 24-hour cycles can also be arranged upon request.
