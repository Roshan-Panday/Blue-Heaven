Bungee FAQs

Is it safe?
Yes, we use imported equipment and follow New Zealand safety standards. Our crew is highly trained. 100% safety record.
What is the weight limit?
Minimum weight is 40kg, Maximum is 110kg. This is strictly enforced for safety.
Can I eat before jumping?
Avoid heavy meals right before the jump. A light snack 2 hours prior is fine. Do not jump on an empty stomach if you have low BP.
Do I get a video?
Yes, HD video and photos are available for an extra charge. You can view them after your jump and decide to buy.
